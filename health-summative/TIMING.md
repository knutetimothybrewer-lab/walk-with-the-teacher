## How to use pilot data

After the 3–5 student pilot (see ROLLOUT.md), compare the students' actual times with this table. If students are consistently faster or slower, change `WPS` or the base seconds in `js/engine/timing.js`, or add or remove items. Then re-run `node tools/timing.js --write`.
