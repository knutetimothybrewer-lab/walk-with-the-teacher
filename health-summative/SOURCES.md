## What is *not* a sourced statistic

* "About 3 in 10 / 4 in 10" and "about 10 percentage points" in explanations are simple rounding of #1.
* The stress-over-two-weeks chart and the Recovery Dial gauges are **illustrations, not data**, and say so on screen.
* The 14-day stress model values are made up for teaching and labeled as such.

## Style note for the safe-messaging approach

Suicide appears only as (a) a CDC statistic in a reading that students can skip, (b) the phrase "talking about wanting to die or disappear" in the red-flags list, and (c) a chat scene in which a friend says they wish they could "disappear." There are no methods, locations, graphic details, or depictions of attempts anywhere. Every safety scene models calm language, "Are you safe right now?", walking to an adult, and not promising secrecy, and the help button is always visible.
