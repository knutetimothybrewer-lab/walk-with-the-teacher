Foundational Knowledge Still Matters

↳ Asking “Can I trust it?” only works if learners have something to check the answer against.

🧠

Subject knowledge lets students

  • Identify errors
  • Ask useful questions
  • Judge AI-generated responses

Product versus understanding

A polished product can look finished. Understanding is the ability to explain why it works.

Example A student cannot reliably evaluate an AI explanation of a scientific experiment without understanding variables and evidence.
