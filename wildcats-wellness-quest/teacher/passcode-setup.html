Teacher passcode setup

This page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and your passcode is never stored. It generates a salted, iterated SHA-256 verifier that you paste into js/teacher-config.js.

Honest limit. This protects the Teacher reset dialog from casual use. It is not real security: anyone who can open browser developer tools can read or change the verifier and the application code. See the README, "Honest technical limits."
