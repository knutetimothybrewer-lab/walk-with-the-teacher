## 5. Misconception distractors by design

- One healthy domain means total wellness (3.1, 6.4, 7.2)
- Lower readings are always better (2.2 heart rate, glucose, BMI)
- Repeating one post counts as independent verification (4.x evidence options)
- Missing one day ruins a habit (3.3)
- A specific goal is automatically fully SMART (3.4, 3.5, 7.1)
- The largest running total means every domain is thriving (6.3, 6.4)
- A single reading is a diagnosis (2.2)
- Ads / AI / polish alone decide credibility (4.x, 7.1)
