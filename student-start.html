Health Summatives: Start Here

Use this page to check your Chromebook and open your assessment. Your teacher will tell you which one is yours today.

1. Before you begin

  • Use Chrome on your school Chromebook, in a normal window (not Incognito).
  • Your teacher will give you a class code out loud or on a slip. It is not on this page.
  • Do not clear your browsing data or sign out in the middle of the assessment. Refreshing the page is safe and your work is saved.
  • There is a 90-minute limit for most assessments, counted from when you press start. Reopening the page does not add time.
  • Stay on the same Chromebook for the whole assessment.
  • If anything in an assessment upsets you, tell your teacher or go see your counselor. You can take a break.

2. Check your Chromebook (30 seconds)

This tests whether your school network lets the assessment reach the place it saves your score. It does not send any of your information.

    3. Open your assessment

    Open only the one your teacher assigned today.

    If something goes wrong

    • Page will not load or says it cannot reach the server: raise your hand. Do not keep retrying. Your teacher can fix it.
    • Your score does not send: take a screenshot of the score page and the completion code, then show your teacher. Later, reopen the same link on the same Chromebook and press "Try sending again".
    • Class code rejected: check for typos and spaces. Codes are not case-sensitive.
