Help

A normal day

  1. Run class: for each period, check the lesson in the box (it suggests the next one), then press Open warm-up. Students enter the class code and their ID.
  2. Watch the count go up. Press Close when everyone is in (or when time is up).
  3. At the end of class press Open exit ticket. Students see their score, their warm-up score, the change, and an explanation of each question.
  4. Open Results for the class, or Students for one student's growth over time.

Reading the numbers

  • Change = exit ticket % minus warm-up %, in points.
  • Growth (g) = the share of the possible improvement a student achieved: (exit − warm-up) ÷ (100 − warm-up). 0.5 means they closed half the gap. It is not shown when the warm-up was already 100%.
  • Class growth uses the class averages the same way, so it is not thrown off by one student.
  • Only students who took both tickets are used for change and growth. Absent students are listed but never pull the averages down.
  • A criterion is flagged Reteach when the class is below the mastery line on the exit ticket or improved by less than 10 points. If the class was already at the mastery line at the warm-up and stayed there, it shows Already strong instead.
