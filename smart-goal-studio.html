What SMART means

S

Specific

What exactly will you do, and how will you do it?

M

Measurable

What number or count shows you are making progress?

A

Achievable

Can you do it with the time, skills and support you have?

R

Relevant

Why does it matter to you, and how does it fit your bigger plans?

T

Time-bound

What is the deadline, and what are the checkpoints on the way?

Same idea, two versions

Fuzzy
I want to get better at math.No finish line, no plan, easy to quit without noticing.
SMART
By December 18, I will raise my Algebra II grade from 72% to 85% by attending tutoring twice a week and doing 3 practice problems every night, because it keeps me on the honors track.
