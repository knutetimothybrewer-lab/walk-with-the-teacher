## B. Interaction map (what students do, and the thinking required)
| Mission | Environment | Interactions (type) | Thinking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 System check | Network field, calm teal | Practice sort (drag/tap), scoring explainer, settings | Learn the controls without grading pressure |
| 1 Brain & Addiction Lab | Violet neural network with moving pulses | Brain hotspot; sequence; matching; **two-panel synapse animation** (particle model); multiple choice; case-file classification with an "insufficient evidence" option; risk/protective sort; claim evaluation | Interpret mechanism, apply distinctions, resist over-diagnosing |
| 2 Nicotine & Vaping Timeline | Teal rising aerosol particles | Match; four-way sort (smoke / aerosol / both / myth); multi-select; **timeline placement**; **graph with predict-then-reveal**, hover/keyboard readouts, series toggles, year comparison, data table; percent-change calculation | Data literacy: what a graph does and does not prove |
| 3 Alcohol Impairment Lab | Amber/red instrument grid with waveforms | **Reaction-time lab** (5 baseline trials, 5 modeled-delay trials, animated stopping lanes, distance table); **BAC model** (drinks, body size, rate, food, elapsed time, uncertainty band); BAC data table; myth/fact/depends sort; scenario pool | Use models and know their limits; evaluate myths |
| 4 Substance Risk Investigation | Soft green attention/radar field | Multi-select; scenario pool with an illustrated timing chart; use/misuse sort; claim evaluation; five-way match including the "cancel out" myth | Non-glamorizing, evidence-based reasoning |
| 5 Overdose Response | Dark blue/red emergency monitor | Hotspot; counterfeit-pill evidence; true/not-true sort; **animated emergency scene** you examine (wake, breathing, lips, distractor objects) with a running response clock; **ordered response builder** with distractors | Recognize, decide immediately, sequence |
| 6 Pressure & Decision Simulator | Drifting message bubbles, rose/blue | Direct/indirect sort; fictional-ad analysis; **refusal builder** (three slots with live message preview); **branching conversation** with four meters and a post-run branch explorer; when-to-get-help multi-select | Short, assertive, safety-first communication |
| 7 Claim Verification Lab | Investigation corkboard with pinned cards | Claim pool (post + source excerpt/graph/headline) with three-way verdict and rewrite or missing-context step; **Evidence Lab** (viral claim, graph, headline, retailer post, two source excerpts) with credibility ranking and verdict | Evaluate evidence rather than label true/false |
| Final: Synthesis case | Navy and gold network | Scenario timeline with irrelevant details; triage ranking; influence sort; counterfeit-pill reasoning; safer-ride multi-select; claim; emergency sequence; consequence matching; protective factors | Transfer across everything |

Interaction types in the bank: multiple choice, multi-select, hotspot, matching, sorting, sequencing, timeline, ordered response-building, slot-based refusal construction, predict-then-reveal graphs, five simulations. Multiple choice is about 41% of items (24 of 58 in a typical version).

## C. Visual concept
"SIGNAL" is an investigation: a pulse line travels through eight nodes, and each mission has its own environment generated as animated SVG (neural network pulses, aerosol particles, instrument waveforms, radar field, ECG sweep, message bubbles, evidence corkboard, converging network). Transitions are full-screen title cards ("signal connecting") that skip on click. Typography pairs Fraunces (display) with Atkinson Hyperlegible (body). Panels sit on semi-opaque dark glass with one accent color per mission; correctness and risk are never color-only (icons and words accompany every state). Motion carries meaning (particles crossing a synapse, a BAC curve redrawing, stopping lanes, a response clock, bubbles appearing in a chat) and stops under reduced-motion settings.
