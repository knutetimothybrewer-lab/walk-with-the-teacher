# Sources and research

*Teacher Mode shows the same information in **Sources and research**. Citations were checked on 2026-10-09. “Checked” means the bibliographic record or abstract was confirmed; it does not mean every claim in a paper was audited.*

Rule used throughout the build: **invented data is labeled FICTIONAL on screen, real research is cited, and nothing is presented as research that is not.** No statistic was made up to sound authoritative.

## What is fictional

- All companies, apps, ads, athletes, teams, players, fans, posts and quotes are invented.
- Sportsbook odds, implied probabilities, team ratings, home-court edge and player stats come from a **disclosed model** (an Elo-style rating formula plus a stated margin), shown to students.
- Viewer-age percentages in the Ad Lab, social-feed counts and the “customer report” are made up for teaching; they are not statistics about any real app.
- Neuroscience Lab curves, reward-prediction-error trials and the darts are simplified teaching models.
- Coin, die, house-edge and 10,000-slip simulations are pseudo-random classroom models. Tokens are pretend; no real money is involved.
