## Timing rationale (a model, not a measurement)

Task text totals about 4,100 words (prompts, options, rows, categories; mission blurbs included), plus roughly 1,500 words of explorer panels students need to scan.

| Component | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Careful reading of task text at ~200 words/minute | ≈ 21 min |
| Scanning explorer panels (tabs students actually open) | ≈ 5–8 min |
| Interaction and decision time (≈ 90 answer interactions, ~8–12 s each, plus thinking on plans and budget) | ≈ 15–20 min |
| Ordinary retries (≈ 15–25% of units use a second attempt, ~40 s each) | ≈ 4–6 min |
| Tutorial and final review (ungraded) | 5 min |
| **Typical total** | **≈ 55–60 min** (plausible range 45–70) |

Why this is uncertain: reading speed varies widely in a 10th-grade class; the three plan tasks (soccer/UV/heat) and the budget challenge are the slowest; students who explore every tab will exceed the estimate. **Developer click-throughs validate that the flow works, not the timing.**

### Where a classroom pilot is still needed
1. Time the slowest 25% of readers and students with accommodations (set `PacingFactor`).
2. Time Module 3 (22 points, 6 units, 11 minutes budgeted) — it is the likeliest to run long.
3. Check reading load of Module 6 (800 words of options) and consider splitting the session across two periods (progress saves and resumes).
4. Observe whether students open the explorers before answering; adjust prompts if not.
5. Review first-attempt accuracy per item in the `Summary` tab for items that are too easy/hard or ambiguous.

Pacing is shown as a guide only. There is no countdown, no cutoff, nothing locks because of time, and animations are skippable and never required.
