Welcome to BodyLab

A fictional person lives in the middle of the screen. Change their health choices and watch what happens inside the body.

  • Sliders (left): exercise, sleep, food, substances, environment. Color tells you the effect: green helps, amber is a warning, red harms.
  • Years living this way: damage and benefits build up over time. Try ⏩ +20 years!
  • 3D body: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Use View buttons to peel away layers: skin → fat → muscle → bones → organs → blood vessels → nerves.
  • 🔬 Microscope (M): turn it on and move the lens over any organ. Scroll or press 1/2 to zoom from cells to molecules. Click to open a big view.
  • Patient chart (right): vital signs, blood tests, body measures and system scores — click any one for its healthy range, what it measures, and what helps or hurts it.
  • Pose: watch the heart rate and breathing change when jogging, lifting, sitting with a screen, or sleeping.
  • 📓 Notebook: save snapshots to compare scenarios side by side.

Remember: metrics are tools for understanding patterns and risk — not labels for a person. One measurement never tells the whole story, and this is a simplified, fictional model. Questions about real health measurements belong with a qualified health professional.
