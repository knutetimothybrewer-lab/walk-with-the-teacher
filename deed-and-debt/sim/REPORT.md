# DEED & DEBT: Simulation Report

Generated by `node sim/simulate.js 500 4 10` on 2026-11-23.
500 seeded CPU-only games (seeds 1-500), 4 players each, 10 Years (Standard difficulty, ballots on).
Each game, the 4 CPU players get 4 different strategies (of 5), and a random career and rent/buy choice.
The engine is loaded straight from `index.html`. Run time: 2.7 s.

## Estimated real classroom time per game

Model: 20s of human decision time per turn (reading cards and choosing happen inside this), plus 6s per doubles re-roll, plus animation time at Normal speed: 1.1s turn banner, 0.9s per dice roll, 0.15s per space hopped, 0.65s per rent payment, 1.1s per deed flip, 0.6s per card flip, 0.25s per building, 10s per auction (30s cap; it closes as soon as every human presses Pass, and instantly when no human can afford the opening bid).
Once per Year: 1.9s Year banner, 15s to read the event card together, 15s for the Annual Report. Per ballot: 30s to read the measure, 4s per private vote, 5s tally.
Career and home picks at the start (about 2.5 min) belong to the 5-minute setup in the lesson plan, so they are not counted here.
