## Scoring

- Every item may be tried three times. Correct on the 1st try earns 100% of the item's points, the 2nd try 85%, the 3rd try 75%, and 0 after three misses. Points are kept to two decimal places (for example 85% of a 3-point item is 2.55).
- Hint 1 is released after the first miss and hint 2 after the second. Hints never name the answer. After the third miss the item locks and the explanation is shown.
- Items with several parts (labels, sorting, ordering, select-all) are all-or-nothing per attempt: every part must be right for the attempt to count. There is no partial credit.
- Grading happens only on the server. The page never receives an answer key.
