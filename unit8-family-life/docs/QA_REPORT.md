## Automated checks (run on every build; the build fails if any fails)

- Exactly 100 points; every item has a positive whole-number point value.
- Every item has a prompt, topic, cognitive tag, one or more objectives, a time estimate, **exactly two non-empty hints**, and an explanation.
- Single-answer MC has **at least five options** and exactly one correct. Select-all items have at least two correct and two incorrect. Classification needs at least five cards, spread across at least two groups. Ordering needs at least four steps.
- Every objective has at least one item. Every chapter is within +/-15% of its time target.
- **Length-bias rule:** in each single-answer item the correct option must be 80-120% of the average distractor length, and it may not be the longest (or shortest) in more than 40% of single-answer items. In select-all items the correct statements must average 75-130% of the wrong ones.
- No `<` or `>` in learner-facing text; hints may not be empty or very long. Reading level (Flesch-Kincaid) is reported per item and warned above 11.5.
- Figures: every label target has a marker in the SVG.
